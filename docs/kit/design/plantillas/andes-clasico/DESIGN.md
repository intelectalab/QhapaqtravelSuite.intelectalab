## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the authority, cultural depth, and precision of a world-class Peruvian inbound tour operator. It balances editorial travel journalism with the transactional clarity required for high-value journey bookings.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Authoritative & Grounded:** Imparts complete logistical confidence, safety, and operational mastery across the Andes, coastal regions, and Amazonian routes.
- **Refined & Curated:** Avoids low-end tourist tropes in favor of an understated luxury perspective—bespoke itineraries, private guides, and architectural heritage.
- **Warm & Luminous:** Echoes high-altitude Andean skies and historic stonework through crisp contrast, deep indigos, and rich solar accents.

### Design Movement
**Editorial Elegance meets Tactile Glassmorphism.** Clean layouts structured by thin stone-like rules, generous vertical breathing room, high-contrast serif headlines for evocative place-making, and frosted translucent overlays for search surfaces and sticky navigation.

## Colors

The palette derives from the coastal Pacific, glacial Andean cordilleras, and pre-Columbian gold craftsmanship.

### Palette Architecture
- **Deep Navy (`#0F2747`):** Primary brand anchor. Used for high-trust foundation layers: sticky headers, authoritative dark section backdrops, major footers, and prominent text hierarchy anchors.
- **Andean Blue (`#1560B8`):** Interactive driver. Applied to body links, active filter states, navigation indicators, secondary utility buttons, and interactive icons.
- **Inca Gold (`#F2B33D`):** High-priority accent. Dedicated strictly to critical conversion surfaces: "Reservar Ahora" primary CTAs, pricing callouts, review star metrics, and urgent highlight badges. Text rendered over Inca Gold must strictly use `#2A1E00` to exceed WCAG AA contrast thresholds (4.5:1+).
- **Ice Blue (`#E3EEFB`):** Surface & tint role. Serves as light backdrop fill for itinerary day-breakouts, highlight callout boxes, tag fills, and soft hover foundations.
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F9FC`):** Primary application canvas to prevent glare and reduce optical fatigue.
- **Line & Border (`#E4E8EF`):** Subtle structural dividing stroke applied consistently across card borders, grid breaks, and table dividers.
- **Ink Primary (`#15202E`):** Maximum contrast reading text on light backgrounds.
- **Slate Secondary (`#4A5568`):** Supporting metadata, route descriptions, duration counters, and subtle utility notes.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Base for elevated cards, modal panels, input controls, and crisp contrast surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast literary authority of **Noto Serif** with the engineered precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Noto Serif:** Reserved exclusively for editorial destinations, journey titles, storytelling narratives, and quote pull-outs. It brings historical dignity without degrading digital legibility.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Governs all interface interaction—navigation bars, availability calendars, booking steps, itinerary day trackers, pricing figures, and microcopy.
- **Localization (Spanish - Peru):** Currency rendering must follow standard Peruvian conventions (`S/ 1,250` or `USD $1,400`). Ensure letter-spacing rules accommodate longer Romance syntax strings without clipping container widths.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column responsive grid balances informational density with expansive photographic framing.

### Breakpoint Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Sticky conversion drawer docked to viewport bottom.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Search widget transforms into a stacked 2x2 matrix.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout, centered max-width container of `1280px`, `margin: auto` with minimum `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 2rem`.

### Vertical Cadence
- Content sections leverage `space-xl` (40px) up to `80px` on desktop viewports to give regional photography and itinerary timelines distinct rhythmic pacing.
- Component-level spacing follows standard 4px/8px incremental units to maintain alignment across calendar date pickers, guest counters, and filter facets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through ambient, cool-toned shadows paired with glassmorphism for floating utilities.

### Surface Elevation Layers
1. **Flat Canvas (`#F7F9FC`):** Base foundation layer.
2. **Resting Card / Container:** `#FFFFFF` fill with a crisp `1px solid #E4E8EF` boundary. Shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(15, 39, 71, 0.04)`.
3. **Interactive Lift / Hover Card:** Shifts upward by `-2px`. Shadow: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 39, 71, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 39, 71, 0.04)`.
4. **Hero Search Engine & Sticky Bar (Glassmorphic):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)`, and ambient shadow `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 39, 71, 0.12)`.
5. **Modal / Booking Overlay:** Deep backdrop `rgba(15, 39, 71, 0.60)` with surface shadow `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 39, 71, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a consistent architectural radius of **12px (`0.75rem`)** across content surfaces, paired with high-radius pill styling for standalone micro-elements.

### Geometry Tokens
- **Core Containers (Cards, Modals, Inputs, Date-pickers):** Fixed at `12px` corner radius. This conveys architectural solidity and modern polish without feeling playful.
- **Pill Elements (Chips, Filter Badges, Counter Indicators):** Fully rounded (`9999px`) for touch ergonomics and contrast against structured cards.
- **Stonework Line Work:** Key card boundaries and dividers always utilize crisp `1px` geometry without heavy strokes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Reservar Ahora", "Confirmar Itinerario"):** Background `#F2B33D` (Inca Gold), text color `#2A1E00` (bold, 15px), border-radius `12px`, padding `14px 28px`. Hover: `#DE9F2B`, subtle shadow lift. Focus-visible: 2px ring `#1560B8` offset by 2px.
- **Secondary Action ("Ver Itinerario", "Consultar Asesor"):** Background `transparent`, border `1.5px solid #1560B8`, text `#1560B8`. Hover: background `#E3EEFB`, border-color `#1560B8`.
- **Ghost / Minimal:** Background `transparent`, text `#0F2747`. Hover: `#E3EEFB` fill.

### Tour Cards
- **Structure:** 12px border radius, 1px solid border `#E4E8EF`, white background.
- **Visual Presentation:** 16:10 ratio aspect ratio image with top corners rounded (12px). Pill badge top-left for duration (`4D / 3N`), price callout bottom-right with Inca Gold accent.
- **Content:** Title in `Noto Serif` (`headline-sm`), metadata row (Altitude, Difficulty, Group Size) in Slate `#4A5568` with crisp line dividers.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Shape & Dimensions:** Fully rounded pill (`9999px`), padding `6px 14px`, text `label-sm`.
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E4E8EF`, text `#4A5568`.
- **Active State:** Background `#E3EEFB`, border `1px solid #1560B8`, text `#1560B8`, font-weight `700`.

### Form Controls & Booking Inputs
- **Base Style:** 12px border radius, height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E4E8EF`, padding `0 16px`, text `#15202E`.
- **Focus State:** Border `#1560B8`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(21, 96, 184, 0.15)`, outline none.
- **Error State:** Border `#D9383A`, ring `rgba(217, 56, 58, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Geometry:** Checkboxes `6px` radius; Radios `50%` round. Size `20px x 20px`.
- **States:** Border `1.5px solid #E4E8EF`. Checked: background `#1560B8`, white checkmark or inner dot.

### Specialized Travel Components
- **Floating Journey Search Bar:** Frosted glass background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, 16px radius, containing segmented fields (Destino, Fechas, Tipo de Experiencia) separated by 1px vertical borders `#E4E8EF`.
- **Timeline Accordion (Day-by-Day Itinerary):** Vertical connector line in `#E3EEFB`, active day indicator node in Deep Navy `#0F2747` with Inca Gold milestone rings. Day summary containers encased in soft Ice Blue surfaces (`#E3EEFB`).
