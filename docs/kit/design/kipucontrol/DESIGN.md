## Brand & Style

The design system projects mission-critical stability, operational clarity, and high-altitude momentum. Built specifically for complex tourism logistics across Latin America—from multi-day Incan treks to boutique Amazonian river cruises—it balances enterprise ERP rigor with the inviting warmth of the travel industry.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern SaaS** precision with high-craft touches reminiscent of Stripe and Linear:
- **Tone:** Authoritative yet energetic, deeply dependable, frictionless, and modern.
- **Visual Character:** Large curved surfaces, pill-shaped action drivers, crisp ink typography, soft atmospheric depth, and purposeful bursts of sunlight gold against oceanic navies.
- **Affiliation Touchpoints:** Discrete, contextual inclusion of sister-brand accents (Cobalt and Coral) to signal network integrations, ecosystem perks, and marketplace connections without cluttering primary ERP workflows.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unmistakable operational hierarchy designed for clarity under heavy operational loads.

### Core Functional Palette
- **Primary Navy (`#0A3A73`):** Defines structure, top-level navigation, primary brand moments, and structural active states.
- **Royal Blue (`#11509A`):** Used for interaction states, link elements, active filters, and primary button hover profiles.
- **Kipu Sun Yellow (`#F4B400`):** High-conversion CTA triggers, critical action buttons, urgent notices, and status highlights. Always paired with Deep Bronze Ink (`#2B1F00`) to guarantee WCAG AAA contrast.
- **Soft Blue Tint (`#EAF3FC`):** Table row selections, active sidebar highlights, badge backgrounds, and secondary focus envelopes.
- **Success Green (`#22A55A`):** Confirmed bookings, finalized wire transfers, active reservations, and healthy system states.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- **App Canvas (`#F7F9FC`):** Soft, anti-glare canvas backing for prolonged desk work.
- **Surface Card (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity elevated workspace containers.
- **Border Line (`#E4E8EF`):** Crisp 1px division lines for tables, split-screens, and inputs.
- **Ink Primary (`#15202E`):** Headers, key metrics, and high-emphasis data points.
- **Slate Body (`#4A5568`):** Body paragraphs, contextual labels, descriptions, and secondary metadata.

### Ecosystem Accents (Sister Brand Integration)
- **Cobalt (`#1D4ED8`):** Direct B2B-to-B2C sync badges and channel manager data feeds.
- **Coral (`#F95738`):** Special inventory callouts, external marketplace promotions, and live fleet alerts.

## Typography

Typography relies uniformly on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, marrying corporate density with geometric approachability.

- **Display & Headlines:** Use tight negative letter spacing (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weight profiles (ExtraBold / Bold) to create punchy, editorial-grade hierarchy for analytics titles, operational hubs, and landing pages.
- **Body Text:** Scaled generously to `17px` with an expansive line height of `1.6` (`27px`) on primary descriptions to eliminate ocular fatigue during dense operational management.
- **Numbers & Data:** Metrics employ tabular figures (`tnum`) for currency, passenger counts, and booking references to avoid column drift across tables.
- **Labels:** System badges and table headers use uppercase `11px` Bold with `+0.06em` tracking for crisp scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a hybrid multi-column responsive system:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Fixed 260px operational sidebar, paired with a 12-column fluid grid, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** Collapsible 72px icon sidebar, 8-column layout, `1.25rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked canvas, bottom app-bar navigation, `1rem` (16px) margins and gutters.

### Spatial Rhythm
Data blocks must avoid cramped interfaces. Dense tabular modules leverage `space-sm` (8px) vertical padding, while analytical KPI cards and booking summary trays enforce `space-lg` (24px) to `space-xl` (40px) of breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated with Linear/Stripe-caliber subtlety, avoiding harsh drop shadows in favor of ambient multi-stop atmospheric elevation.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** `#F7F9FC` with optional 1px border (`#E4E8EF`).
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Panels):**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid #E4E8EF`
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(10, 58, 115, 0.03), 0 4px 12px rgba(10, 58, 115, 0.04)`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Flyouts & Filter Dropdowns):**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(10, 58, 115, 0.08)`
  - Shadow: `0 4px 6px -2px rgba(10, 58, 115, 0.04), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(10, 58, 115, 0.08)`
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Itinerary Drawers):**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Shadow: `0 16px 48px -8px rgba(10, 58, 115, 0.14), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(10, 58, 115, 0.06)`
- **Level 4 (Elevated CTAs / Active Drag Elements):**
  - Yellow CTA ambient glow: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(244, 180, 0, 0.35)`

## Shapes

The interface embraces high-radius geometric harmony, pairing sweeping rounded enclosures with pill-shaped control nodes:

- **Cards and Major Panels:** Standardized to a generous `24px` (`rounded-xl` equivalent in this system's spatial tier), softening dense analytical content.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Badges, Search Inputs, Chips):** Standardized to fully rounded pill forms (`9999px`).
- **Data Inputs & Form Controls:** Standardized to `12px` to preserve structural alignment alongside square table columns and fieldsets.
- **Nested Inner Surfaces:** Nested cards or metric tiles inside primary cards use `16px` to maintain concentric boundary harmony.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Operational:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `44px` (regular) or `52px` (hero/checkout), background `#F4B400`, text `#2B1F00`, font weight 700. Hover: `#E0A500`, subtle upward translate `(-1px)`, yellow ambient glow shadow.
- **Corporate Action:** Pill-shaped, background `#0A3A73`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600. Hover: `#11509A`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Pill-shaped, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E4E8EF`, text `#15202E`. Hover: `#F7F9FC` with border `#0A3A73`.

### Cards
- Radius: `24px`. Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #E4E8EF`.
- Padding: `24px` desktop, `16px` mobile.
- Header: Divided optionally with clean `1px solid #F7F9FC` divider, housing title, subtitle, and an action chip.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Height: `46px`. Corner radius: `12px`.
- Border: `1.5px solid #E4E8EF`. Background: `#FFFFFF`. Text: `#15202E`.
- Focus State: Border color `#11509A`, ring `3px solid #EAF3FC`.
- Placeholder: `#4A5568` at 60% opacity.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 12px`, text style `body-sm`.
- **Confirmed / Paid:** BG `#E8F7EE`, Text `#1A7A42`, Dot `#22A55A`.
- **Pending / Action Required:** BG `#FEF7E6`, Text `#7A5A00`, Dot `#F4B400`.
- **Partner Sync (Rutas Libres):** BG `rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.08)`, Text `#1D4ED8`, border `1px solid rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.2)`.

### Lists & Tables
- Table headers: Uppercase `label-caps`, color `#4A5568`, background `#F7F9FC`, height `40px`.
- Rows: Background `#FFFFFF`, border-bottom `1px solid #E4E8EF`, vertical padding `14px`. Hover state: `#F7F9FC`. Active/Selected row: `#EAF3FC`.

### Itinerary & Manifest Timeline (Domain-Specific)
- Vertical node tracker: 2px connecting rule (`#E4E8EF`) with 12px pill nodes.
- Passenger status chips inline, guide assignment avatars with 2px white borders, and real-time departure badges.
